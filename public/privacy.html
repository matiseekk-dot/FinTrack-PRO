Privacy Policy

Sidegig · Last updated: 7 October 2026

In short: Sidegig stores what you enter on your device. If you sign in with Google, it is also synced to your own space in Google Firebase, so you can use it on several devices. We don't sell, share or analyse your data, there are no ads and no analytics trackers.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your personal data is the creator of Sidegig ("we"). Contact: matiseekk@gmail.com.

2. What data we process

2.1 Account

You can use Sidegig without an account — then everything stays only on your device. If you sign in with Google, we receive your user ID (UID), email address and, optionally, your name and profile picture. We never see your Google password.

2.2 What you enter

Everything you type into the app: entries (amounts, dates, descriptions, currency), and the data of the modules you use — items you resell, gigs and clients, collections and their catalogue, bets, investment positions, trips (including names of travel companions you add), hobbies and subscriptions, goals and settings. It is stored on your device (encrypted in the browser storage) and, if you are signed in, synced to Google Firebase Firestore under your account.

We do not ask for and do not store bank logins, card numbers, account numbers or government ID numbers. Please don't type them into descriptions.

If you add other people (e.g. trip companions), enter only what you need — usually a first name.

2.3 Services the app contacts for you

Some features fetch public information directly from your device. These services see your IP address and the request itself, not your financial data:

  • NBP (National Bank of Poland) — exchange rates.
  • CoinGecko — crypto and gold prices (only the coin names you track).
  • Open Library, Google Books — book details for the ISBN you scan or type.
  • MusicBrainz, Open Products Facts — record and product details for the barcode you scan or type.
  • Discogs — only if you connect it: your Discogs username and, optionally, your personal token, which stay only on your device and are never synced or backed up.

2.4 Camera

The camera is used only when you scan barcodes. The video is processed on your device; no image is stored or sent anywhere.

2.5 No analytics, no ads

Sidegig has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. If you send us feedback from the app, your email app opens with your message and, if you agree, a list of recent app errors — nothing is sent without you pressing send.

3. Why we process it

  • To provide the app and sync it between your devices (performance of the service — Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • To answer your messages (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR).

4. Who has access

  • You. Firebase security rules let only your signed-in account read and write your data.
  • Google (Firebase) as our processor, under the Firebase Data Processing Terms. Data is stored in Google Cloud (region europe-west3, Frankfurt).
  • Us. As owners of the Firebase project we technically have administrative access to the database. We don't look at your data and use this access only when you ask us to (e.g. to delete your account).

We don't pass your data to advertisers, data brokers or anyone else.

5. How long we keep it

As long as you use the app. In Settings → Clear all data you delete everything on the device and in the cloud. To also remove your sign-in record, email us — we will do it within 7 days. Without an account your data exists only on your device: clearing the browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete and port your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to complain to a data protection authority (in Poland: uodo.gov.pl). You can export everything yourself in Settings → Export to Excel.

7. Security

  • Encrypted connection (HTTPS/TLS) to the cloud.
  • Data on the device encrypted in the browser storage; optional PIN lock in the app.
  • Firebase security rules: each user can access only their own data.

8. Children

Sidegig is not intended for children under 16. The Betting module is available only to adults (18+).

9. Changes

We will announce important changes in the app. The current version is always at this address.

10. Contact

matiseekk@gmail.com

Niektóre funkcje pobierają publiczne informacje bezpośrednio z Twojego urządzenia. Te usługi widzą Twój adres IP i samo zapytanie, a nie Twoje dane finansowe:

Aparat działa tylko podczas skanowania kodów kreskowych. Obraz jest analizowany na urządzeniu — nic nie jest zapisywane ani wysyłane.

Sidegig nie ma reklam ani narzędzi analitycznych i śledzących. Gdy wysyłasz zgłoszenie z aplikacji, otwiera się Twoja poczta z wiadomością i — jeśli się zgodzisz — listą ostatnich błędów aplikacji. Nic nie zostaje wysłane, dopóki sam nie klikniesz „Wyślij”.
